Consequently, an app configured with a clock uses that clock for the engine/store and wall time for metrics timestamps and cadence. A fake-clock app can keep metrics apparently fresh because their timestamps lie in its future, and advancing its clock does not trigger scrapes. This violates the new collector environment’s clock contract and weakens integration-test evidence.
